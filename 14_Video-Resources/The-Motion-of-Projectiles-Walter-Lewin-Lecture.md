## Why Watch This

Walter Lewin's legendary MIT lecture demonstrates, live, that horizontal and vertical motion are independent — the single idea that unlocks all projectile problems. Longer than a typical clip, but the demos are worth it.

## What It Covers

- Using SUVAT separately in each direction
- Live demonstrations, including the famous monkey-and-hunter idea
