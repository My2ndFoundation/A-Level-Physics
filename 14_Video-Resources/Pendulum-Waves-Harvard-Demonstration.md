## Why Watch This

Fifteen pendulums of increasing length drift in and out of phase, creating mesmerising travelling- and standing-wave illusions. It shows viscerally how period depends on length and how phase differences accumulate.

## What It Covers

- Dependence of pendulum period on length
- Phase differences between oscillators
- Emergent wave-like patterns from simple harmonic motion
